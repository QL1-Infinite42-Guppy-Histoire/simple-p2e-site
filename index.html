How to play

Tap to stack

A block slides back and forth. Tap to drop it — the part that lands on the stack below stays, the rest falls away. Miss completely and the game ends.

PERFECT combos

Land a block exactly and it counts as PERFECT: that floor scores double, and consecutive PERFECTs multiply further (up to ×4).

Score = points

Every floor has a point value. Your best score of the day becomes your points for the day.

Rankings

Daily rankings by best score, and season rankings by the total of all your plays. Seasons run in half-months (1st–15th / 16th–end, JST).

Plays

· Unlimited plays while rewards are being prepared. Once QOM payouts start, free plays are planned to be 3 a day.
· Extra plays are planned to cost ZAP on 42 Chain: pay 10 ZAP × a tier (×1 to ×5) and that play's score is multiplied by the same tier. Of each payment, 30% is burned and 70% goes into the season reward pool. (Not purchasable yet — the receiving wallet is being prepared.)

Rewards (preparing)

The planned rate is 1 point = 1 QOM, paid as a hybrid: a small per-score payout for everyone, plus rank bonuses from the season pool for top players. Rankings stay provisional until scores are reviewed, and only then are bonuses granted. Payouts are not active yet.

Guppy's Giveaway was cancelled on November 17, 2026 — its distribution role has moved to this game.

Fair play

Scores are not self-reported. The server replays your taps and recomputes the true score, so a forged score cannot be recorded.
